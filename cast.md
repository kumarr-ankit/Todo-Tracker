# Indian Caste System Simplified (Complete Surnames)

This document explains the Indian caste system in a simplified and detailed way, showing the connection between Varna → Community Umbrella → Example Surnames with comprehensive surname listings under each group.

---

## 1. Varna

The four traditional Varnas are:

1. *Brahmin*: Priestly / scholarly class
2. *Kshatriya*: Warrior / ruling class
3. *Vaishya*: Merchant / trader class
4. *Shudra*: Laborer / artisan / service class

## 4. Notes & Disclaimers

1. *Surnames do not strictly indicate caste* in modern usage; social mobility, adoption of surnames, and regional practices have led to significant overlap.

2. *Official caste classification* (General/OBC/SC/ST/EBC) varies by state and sub-caste. The same surname may be in different categories in different states.

3. *This guide is for educational and reference purposes only*, not for legal or official caste verification.

4. *Many surnames are adopted across communities* for social mobility, prestige, or political reasons (especially "Singh," "Sharma," "Verma," "Gupta," "Patel").

5. *State-specific variations exist extensively*. A community considered SC in one state may be OBC or General in another.

6. *Religious conversion affects classification*: Dalit Christians and Dalit Muslims do not receive SC status in most states, though Dalit Sikhs and Buddhists do.

7. *Modern identity politics* have led to movements for reclassification, with many communities seeking OBC or SC status for reservations.

8. *Tribal (Adivasi/ST) communities* are not included in the Varna system but form a separate category with their own distinct identities and surnames.

9. *Inter-caste marriages and urban migration* have made caste identification by surname increasingly unreliable.

10. *Regional language variations* mean the same community might be known by different names (e.g., Scheduled Castes called "Harijan," "Dalit," "Adi Dravida," "Depressed Classes" in different regions).
